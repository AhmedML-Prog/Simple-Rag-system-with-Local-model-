# GlobalTech Solutions Employee Handbook

## Welcome Message
Welcome to GlobalTech Solutions! This handbook outlines our company policies, procedures, and guidelines effective January 15, 2024. Our founder Alex Morgan established the business in Toronto, Canada, with a mission to provide innovative software solutions for healthcare organizations.

## Company Overview
GlobalTech Solutions serves Canadian healthcare providers through custom application development and cloud services. We value collaboration, continuous learning, and work-life balance. All employees receive an annual training allowance of $150.

## Work Schedule
Standard schedule: Monday-Friday 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM with lunch break 12:00-1:00 PM. Saturday available only for urgent client calls (max 4 hours). No Friday work on holidays.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)
Employees earn 2 days PTO monthly after 3 months tenure. Accrues at rate of 0.5 day per full month worked. Can be carried over up to 15 days maximum.

## Annual Leave
Minimum 15 calendar days annual leave after first year. Requests must be submitted 6 weeks in advance via HR portal.

## Sick Leave
First day sick: no pay. Subsequent days: paid at 70% salary. Maximum 28 consecutive sick days per year.

## Holiday Policy
Company holiday schedule available annually. Employees receive 13 additional days PTO for company holidays.

## Salary & Benefits
Base salary $5,200 CAD monthly (paid bi-weekly). Health insurance premium $25/month employee share. Dental/vision benefits provided. Vested after 1 year.

## Equipment Usage
All personal devices approved. Company-issued laptop ($999) must be encrypted. Expense reimbursements within 30 days with receipts.

## Remote Work Allowance
Remote workers receive $75/month stipend for home office setup. Requires security clearance and minimum 4-hour daily availability.

## Code of Conduct
Professional conduct expected at all times. Zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination. Violations may result in disciplinary action.

## Data Security
Employees must encrypt data on personal devices. Use company VPN for external access. Report security breaches immediately to IT support.

## Safety Compliance
Wear safety gear during construction tasks. Report hazards via mobile app within 1 hour of discovery.

## Reporting Concerns
Submit concerns through HR hotline (416-555-0198) or email ethics@globaltech.ca. Anonymous reporting allowed.
